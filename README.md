### 🚀 Projects:

#### 🍲 [Meal Planner](https://github.com/sofiamaggioni/meal_organization)
Personal app that automates meal planning by rotating weekly meals through your own recipes (season-aware) and generates a shopping list from the confirmed plan. Built with Next.js and Supabase (Postgres, auth, storage).

#### ✈️ [Travel Planner](https://github.com/sofiamaggioni/Travel_planner)
A web app for organizing trips: accommodations, transport, daily itinerary, shared expenses, packing lists and to-dos. Python/Flask + SQLAlchemy backend, with Google Maps and AviationStack integration for flight tracking.

#### 🦎 [Madagascar Tour Guide Website](https://tiaray-madagascar-tour.onrender.com/)
A multilingual website built for our Madagascar tour guide, they were amazing with us and I wanted to give them the online presence it deserved. Not in my own repo but I was responsible for the **frontend, UI/UX design, and deployment**. Built with Node.js.

#### 🌍 [Il Mondo di Sofia](https://ilmondodisofia.onrender.com/)
My personal travel blog, where I share detailed itineraries, budgets, hotel picks, and practical guides from my trips (USA, Thailand, Madagascar, Scotland, Normandy & Brittany, and more). Rather than building it on a generic blogging platform, I built it from scratch with Astro to have full control over the design and the reading experience.
